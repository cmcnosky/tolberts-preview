OWNER REVIEW · NOVEMBER 5, 2026

Keep it useful
after launch.

A restaurant website is only as dependable as its menu, hours, show listings, and contact routes. This plan turns the working demonstration into a system the Tolbert’s team can own and keep current.

Current status: this is a public, independently hosted redesign preview. It is marked noindex and has not changed the restaurant’s official domain, CMS, ordering, analytics, or contact systems.

What this review can decide

  • Keep the directionConfirm that the guest experience, visual character, and three primary tasks are worth taking into production.
  • Name the sourcesIdentify the approved source and staff owner for menu, hours, shows, photographs, and inquiries.
  • Price the launchDefine the integrations, migration, testing, training, support period, and acceptance criteria before setting the fee.

What the working preview demonstrates

  • A complete HTML menu with prices, categories, search, and shareable filtered links.
  • A music finder that reads the restaurant’s public event feed, removes ended shows, labels freshness, and provides a dated fallback and official-calendar route when live data is unavailable.
  • Individual show planning with menu, phone, directions, a shareable link, and a calendar reminder.
  • Today’s posted hours in Grapevine’s time zone, plus full weekly hours and visit information.
  • Clear handoffs to the existing SpotOn ordering, telephone, directions, newsletter, and social destinations.
  • A group-inquiry tool that prepares an email for the guest to review and send. It is not a delivered web submission.

The production sequence

  1. Confirm the facts and owners. Reconcile prices, ingredients, hours, and show details. The Chili Verde disagreement between published sources is one concrete issue that needs an owner decision.
  2. Connect the approved systems. Preserve SpotOn, analytics, newsletter, event publishing, and existing contact delivery unless the owners deliberately replace them.
  3. Test the real operation. Verify actual inquiry delivery and external handoffs. Test physical phones, major browsers, keyboard and screen-reader use, enlarged text, and representative network conditions.
  4. Migrate with a recovery path. Preserve or redirect useful URLs, publish an identifiable version, verify the official domain, and document backup and rollback.
  5. Train with a real update. Have the named staff editor change an actual menu item, special-hours exception, or show before handoff is accepted.

Measure the first 30 days

Record a comparable pre-launch baseline, then measure menu searches, show-detail views, directions, tap-to-call, order handoffs, calendar saves, and completed inquiries. Review broken paths, content-update effort, traffic mix, and event timing alongside those actions.

These are activity signals. They are not completed purchases or visits, and the preview contains no analytics collector or revenue result. Any financial case should use project cost and verified business inputs supplied by Tolbert’s.
