What changed, and why it is useful

  • A restaurant-specific opening. The Main Street sign and stage photographs, Tolbert’s logo, 1976/2006 history, and daily artist panel establish the food and music identity. Menu, music, posted hours, and a seating telephone are available near the top.
  • A usable web menu. All 71 entries in eight categories are readable HTML, with search, categories, prices, reset controls, shareable query states, and the original PDF. Menu search preserves boundaries between item fields, preventing false matches such as “Tail” plus “Served” becoming “Tails.”
  • A night-out planning tool. A dedicated music page offers Tonight, the current or next Friday–Sunday weekend, a date selector, and artist/style search. Each show links directly to its details, menu, directions, seating phone number, sharing, and a calendar file.
  • Honest calendar states. The page reads the restaurant’s existing public event feed, hides ended shows, and labels live checks or the dated saved fallback. An official-calendar link remains available. It does not claim a table, ticket, current cover charge, or guaranteed availability.
  • Clear mobile actions. Menu, Music, Visit, and Order remain in the bottom action bar. The Visit section gathers weekly hours, directions, parking, calling, and common questions.

Browser checks performed

  • Responsive layout: home, menu, music, proposal, and operations at 320, 390, 768, and 1280 CSS viewport widths. No horizontal page overflow or missing images in the 20 recorded observations. This is desktop Chrome viewport emulation, not physical-device testing.
  • Menu: category filtering, text search, clear/reset behavior, shared query state, and an empty dietary-query result with guidance to contact the restaurant. Donkey Tails returns two entries: the dish and the Sampler Platter description. The Chili category shows five entries.
  • Music: the weekend filter shows the November 6–8 listings; adding “Floyd” narrows it to Texas Floyd. Selecting November 6 shows Velvet Love Box. An invalid date parameter does not break the listing.
  • Show links: an exact show URL opens the correct dialog and survives reload. Browser Back closes it, Forward reopens it, and Close/Escape preserve the underlying filter state. Focus returns to the originating show control where applicable.
  • Sharing and reminders: the copy action reports success and retains a visible URL fallback. A Texas Floyd calendar file was downloaded and inspected: November 7 at 9 pm Central becomes November 8 at 03:00 UTC, with the listed 11:59 pm end at 05:59 UTC.
  • Mobile navigation: More opens the additional destinations. Choosing Visit from the root homepage closes navigation, updates the fragment, and focuses the Visit section.
  • Group inquiry: sample details create the correct reviewable email draft. Escape closes the dialog and returns focus to the opener. No message was sent and no delivery claim is made.
  • Live event data: the browser reached the restaurant’s public feed and displayed a successful live-check label. Feed access is an external dependency and may change.

What remains before an official launch

The preview is publicly accessible and marked noindex. It has not replaced the restaurant’s domain or systems. Menu details need owner approval, including the Chili Verde ingredient disagreement between the PDF and ordering service. Photo and brand permissions, the official CMS workflow, special-hours handling, analytics, redirects, structured data, and actual inquiry delivery require an agreed production scope.

Physical phones, other browser engines, screen-reader journeys, representative network conditions, actual orders, delivered inquiries, and completed reservations have not been verified here. A 30-day measurement plan is proposed; no analytics collector or conversion experiment has been installed in this preview.

The working design supports a specific claim: it provides more direct, testable ways to complete the examined guest tasks. Whether Tolbert’s guests prefer it, and whether it earns incremental revenue, require real users and post-launch evidence.
