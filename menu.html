A few menu details.

Make it yours. Burger and sandwich add-ons include sautéed onions or peppers, poblano peppers, mushrooms, jalapeños, fried egg, guacamole, avocado, bacon, and chili. Ask for available options and prices.

A gluten-free menu is available on request. Fried items are cooked in beef tallow. Tell the team about allergies and dietary needs.

Minimum 20% gratuity for parties of 8 or more. Raw or undercooked meat, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs can increase foodborne-illness risk.
